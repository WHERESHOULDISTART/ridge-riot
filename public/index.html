  • Trick + Down backflip · Trick + Up frontflip

  • Trick alone no-hander · Kick L superman · Kick R tabletop
  • Air time, clean tricks and hitting rivals fill the BOOST bar (it also creeps up by itself). Hold the boost button to burn it.
  • Land it straight for points. Land sideways and you eat dirt.
